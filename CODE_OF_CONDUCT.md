# Code of Conduct

We want RepoLens to be a welcoming project for everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests and discussions.

**Do:** be respectful and patient, give specific and kind feedback, assume good intent, and accept that people have different experience.

**Do not:** harass, insult or demean anyone; share someone's private information; or post spam or unrelated promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and close or lock threads, that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile (github.com/gnsaddy).

This is a short statement in the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
