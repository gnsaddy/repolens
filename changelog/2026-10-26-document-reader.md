# Read documents in the Knowledge page

### Added
- A document reader in Knowledge → Documents (**Read**, or click the name): contents list, one section (page) at a time or all together, find in the document with snippets, text size 14 to 22 px, full screen, download as Markdown, and open in the wiki. It uses the existing page endpoint, so no new API.
- `docs/architecture.md` explains, step by step and with the real settings, how a PDF is extracted, stored, split, embedded, connected in the graph, searched and answered from, and where it falls short.
