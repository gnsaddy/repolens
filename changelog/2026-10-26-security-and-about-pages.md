# Public Security and About pages

### Added
- `/security`: a public page (no sign-in) with a table of everything that can leave the server and when, plain statements on access, secrets, the AI's limits and the audit log, how to verify the claims, honest limits, a private way to report a vulnerability, and the official source.
- `/about`: a public page on what RepoLens is, what it does, how it is meant to behave, where it stands, who built it, and how to get involved.
- Links to both from the sign-in screen (beside the form and in the side panel), the sidebar footer and Settings → About. The pages stay readable when signed in and offer a way back into the app.

### Changed
- The sign-in panel's "How we think about security" now opens the in-app Security page. SECURITY.md describes the private reporting route and points to the new pages.
