# Audit log page

### Added
- Audit log (sidebar, admins only): every sensitive action with who did it, the target, and whether it was allowed or refused with the reason; filters for area, outcome, person and text; paging; CSV download.
- `GET /api/audit`, `/api/audit/facets` and `/api/audit/export.csv`.

### Security
- Targets and details are masked for secrets before they leave the server; CSV cells that start with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` are prefixed so spreadsheets do not run them as formulas; reading or exporting the log is itself recorded.
