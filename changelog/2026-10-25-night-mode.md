# Night mode

### Added
- Night mode: a Night / Day button in the sidebar and a choice of "Match my device", "Day" or "Night" on your Profile. The default follows the device setting; a choice is remembered in this browser only.
- Every colour pair was checked for readable contrast in night mode (body text, muted text, links, badges and warnings all at least 4.5:1); links and active tabs use a lighter indigo on dark cards. The sidebar and the white text on brand buttons look the same in both modes.
