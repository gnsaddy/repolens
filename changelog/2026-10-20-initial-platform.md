# Initial platform

### Added
- Evidence-backed root-cause investigations (LangGraph), hybrid retrieval over code, documents, commits and pull requests, and a typed knowledge graph.
- DeepMind Wiki with README, text, Confluence, Jira, Slack, Notion, Google Docs and SharePoint sources, drift alerts and reviewable edit proposals.
- Change-risk scoring, ownership and bus factor, investigations from CI logs.
- Per-repository access control, single sign-on and SCIM, a policy gateway with an audit log.
- Docker images, a compose stack, Kubernetes manifests and CI/CD.
